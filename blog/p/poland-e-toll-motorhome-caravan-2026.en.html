Poland’s e TOLL Trap for Motorhomes and Caravans: What Changes on 21 September 2026

Current Poland e TOLL rules for motorhomes, cars towing caravans and special camping vehicles, including the 21 September 2026 change and rates.

Updated 24 August 2026. If you are taking a motorhome or towing a caravan through Poland, remember two dates. Until 20 September 2026, a vehicle combination can enter e TOLL when its combined maximum authorised mass exceeds 3.5 tonnes. From 21 September 2026, a towing vehicle with a maximum authorised mass of up to 3.5 tonnes will be exempt even with a trailer. A motorhome that itself exceeds 3.5 tonnes remains liable on the e TOLL road network.

This distinction matters. A 3.49 tonne motorhome, a 2.4 tonne car towing a 1.5 tonne caravan and a 4.1 tonne special purpose motor caravan may all look like camping traffic. The Polish toll system sees three different cases.

The Polish tax administration has published an official notice confirming the exemption from 21 September 2026. Do not apply it early. A planned law and a live toll account are not the same thing.

MAM is not today’s scale weight

MAM means maximum authorised mass, the legal figure recorded in the registration documents. Under the rules valid until 20 September, the trigger for a combination is the sum of the towing vehicle’s MAM and the trailer’s MAM. An empty caravan does not disappear from that calculation.

Check the registration papers for both units before entering Poland. The official e TOLL vehicle classifier also explains liability and rate groups. Length, beds and a tasteful pop top are not weight categories.

What applies until 20 September 2026

Vehicles and combinations exceeding 3.5 tonnes MAM, plus buses regardless of mass, must use e TOLL on charged roads managed by GDDKiA. That includes an ordinary car towing a caravan when the sum of the two authorised masses exceeds 3.5 tonnes.

Example: a car has a MAM of 2400 kg and the caravan 1500 kg. The total is 3900 kg. Until 20 September the combination needs e TOLL on a covered route even if its actual weight on the day is lower.

What changes on 21 September 2026

Vehicles with a MAM up to 3.5 tonnes towing a trailer will cease to be subject to the electronic charge. Their vehicle records will be removed, and accounts used exclusively for those vehicles will be closed. The change covers cars, light vans and motorhomes within the 3.5 tonne limit when towing.

It does not exempt a heavy motorhome. If the motorhome itself is rated at 3850, 4000 or 4250 kg, it remains liable. Adding or removing a bike rack does not rewrite the registration document.

Does special purpose motor caravan status create an exemption

No automatic exemption follows from the description “special purpose motor caravan”. The vehicle’s own MAM remains decisive. A motor caravan up to 3.5 tonnes is outside e TOLL, while one above 3.5 tonnes is liable on the charged network. After 21 September, the new trailer exemption helps a light towing vehicle, not a heavy self propelled motorhome.

How to use e TOLL legally

  1. Create an Internet Customer Account and register the correct vehicle.
  2. Enter the EURO class and registration details accurately.
  3. Choose the free e TOLL PL app, an OBU or a ZSL device.
  4. Fund a prepaid account before entering a charged section.
  5. Confirm that location data is being transmitted.
  6. If your still liable vehicle tows a trailer, activate the trailer setting.

Ordinary tourists cannot simply settle a forgotten journey weeks later. Retrospective registration is reserved for narrowly defined special journeys after a location device failure, not for a family caravan trip.

Recovering money after the September change

If your account was used only for a light vehicle with a trailer, you may apply for a refund of unused prepaid funds or a cash security. The application window is six months from 21 September 2026. Unclaimed funds will not be refundable after that period and will pass to Poland’s National Road Fund.

Five practical examples

  1. 3490 kg motorhome without trailer: no e TOLL before or after the change.
  2. 2200 kg car plus 1400 kg caravan: e TOLL until 20 September on covered roads, exempt from 21 September.
  3. 3500 kg van plus 750 kg trailer: the same change applies because the towing vehicle is within 3.5 tonnes.
  4. 3850 kg motorhome: e TOLL remains required.
  5. 4250 kg special purpose motor caravan with trailer: e TOLL remains required because the towing vehicle itself exceeds 3.5 tonnes.

e TOLL is not every road charge in Poland

e TOLL covers designated roads managed by GDDKiA. Concession motorway charges, parking fees and other operator charges must be checked separately. Being exempt from e TOLL does not make every Polish motorway or car park free.

Departure checklist

  • check whether your travel date is before or after 21 September
  • open the current charged road map
  • confirm the EURO emissions class
  • check account balance and device status when liable
  • save confirmations and a backup route

Frequently asked questions

Does a caravan need e TOLL in Poland in 2026

Until 20 September, yes when the combination’s total MAM exceeds 3.5 tonnes and it uses a covered road. From 21 September, a towing vehicle up to 3.5 tonnes with a trailer is exempt.

Does a 4.25 tonne motorhome need e TOLL

Yes. Its own MAM exceeds 3.5 tonnes, so the new light vehicle and trailer exemption does not apply.

Does actual weight decide liability

No. The threshold uses maximum authorised mass from the documents.

Is the e TOLL PL app free

Yes, but the vehicle must still be registered correctly, data transmission must work and the account must have funds when charges apply.

This guide reflects rules verified on 24 August 2026. Check the official e TOLL website immediately before travel because the road network and rates can change.
