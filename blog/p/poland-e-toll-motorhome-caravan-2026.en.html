Poland e TOLL After 21 September 2026: Cars with Trailers Are Exempt

Since 21 September 2026, cars up to 3.5 t towing a trailer are exempt from Poland’s e TOLL. Check motorhome rules and refund deadlines.

Updated 22 September 2026. The new rules are now in force. Since 21 September 2026, a car, van or motorhome with its own maximum authorised mass of up to 3.5 tonnes does not use Poland’s e TOLL merely because it is towing a trailer. The combined MAM may exceed 3.5 tonnes. A motorhome whose own MAM exceeds 3.5 tonnes remains liable on the e TOLL road network.

This answers the common searches “Poland e TOLL trailer rules 2026”, “does a caravan need e TOLL in Poland” and “Poland road toll for a car with a caravan”. The system now focuses on the towing vehicle’s own MAM for this exemption. A trailer no longer turns a light car into an e TOLL vehicle, but it does not make a 3850 kg motorhome magically lighter.

The Polish National Revenue Administration confirms the exemption, removal of affected vehicle records and the six month refund period in its official e TOLL notice. The legal basis is the Act of 29 May 2026 published in Poland’s Journal of Laws.

Does the combined MAM of car and caravan still matter

For a towing vehicle with its own MAM up to 3.5 tonnes, the combined MAM no longer triggers e TOLL. A 2400 kg car towing a 1500 kg caravan forms a 3900 kg combination, yet it has been exempt since 21 September 2026. Driving licence, towing, actual weight and axle load rules still apply. A toll exemption is not a weight loss programme for the outfit.

MAM is the figure in the registration papers, not the result from a convenient weighbridge visit after emptying the fridge. If the vehicle itself is above 3.5 tonnes, use the official e TOLL vehicle classifier. For a heavy vehicle, the trailer still affects the correct weight and rate category.

Which motorhomes still pay e TOLL in Poland

The exemption does not cover a motorhome rated at 3850, 4000 or 4250 kg. It must still be registered and its journeys must be settled on charged sections. The same applies to a heavy vehicle towing a trailer and to buses under their relevant rules.

The description “special purpose motor caravan” creates no separate exemption. The vehicle’s own MAM remains decisive. A motorhome up to 3.5 tonnes is outside e TOLL, with or without a trailer. A motorhome above 3.5 tonnes remains inside the system.

How to use e TOLL legally

  1. Create an Internet Customer Account and register the correct vehicle.
  2. Enter the EURO class and registration details accurately.
  3. Choose the free e TOLL PL app, an OBU or a ZSL device.
  4. Fund a prepaid account before entering a charged section.
  5. Confirm that location data is being transmitted.
  6. If your still liable vehicle tows a trailer, activate the trailer setting.

Ordinary tourists cannot simply settle a forgotten journey weeks later. Retrospective registration is reserved for narrowly defined special journeys after a location device failure, not for a family caravan trip.

e TOLL refund deadline: 21 March 2027

If the account was used only for a light vehicle with a trailer, unused prepaid funds and a cash security can be reclaimed. The request must be filed within six months of 21 September 2026, which means no later than 21 March 2027. Use the Internet Customer Account or the official e TOLL contact form. After the deadline, the money is not refundable and passes to Poland’s National Road Fund.

Five examples under the current rules

  1. 2200 kg car plus 1400 kg caravan: no e TOLL, although combined MAM is 3600 kg.
  2. 3500 kg van plus 750 kg trailer: no e TOLL because the towing vehicle does not exceed 3.5 tonnes.
  3. 3490 kg motorhome without a trailer: no e TOLL.
  4. 3850 kg motorhome: e TOLL remains required on charged roads.
  5. 4250 kg special purpose motor caravan with a trailer: e TOLL remains required and the trailer affects the rate category.

e TOLL is not every road charge in Poland

e TOLL covers designated roads managed by GDDKiA. Concession motorway charges, parking fees and other operator charges must be checked separately. Being exempt from e TOLL does not make every Polish motorway or car park free.

Checklist after the rule change

  • check the towing vehicle’s own MAM in the registration papers
  • do not start e TOLL solely because a vehicle up to 3.5 t is towing a trailer
  • for a vehicle above 3.5 t, confirm EURO class, trailer category and the current charged road map
  • check the old account balance and request a refund by 21 March 2027
  • save the refund confirmation and travel documents

Frequently asked questions about e TOLL and trailers

Does a caravan need e TOLL in Poland after 21 September 2026

No, provided the towing vehicle has a MAM up to 3.5 tonnes. The combined MAM may exceed 3.5 tonnes without triggering e TOLL.

Does a 4.25 tonne motorhome need e TOLL

Yes. Its own MAM exceeds 3.5 tonnes, so the exemption for light vehicles towing trailers does not apply.

What is the deadline for reclaiming e TOLL money

A claim linked to the trailer exemption must be filed no later than 21 March 2027.

Are all Polish motorways free for a car towing a caravan

No. The exemption concerns e TOLL. Charges from other motorway operators, parking fees and other payments must be checked separately.

The rules and official sources were verified on 22 September 2026. The charged road network and rates for vehicles that remain liable may change.
